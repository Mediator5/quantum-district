## 7. Speed / AI section

Theme: **Lightest 1**.

- Eyebrow: `WHY CLIENTS MOVE FASTER WITH US`
- Heading 2: `Answers in minutes. Momentum in days.`
- Intro: `Most consulting relationships lose two weeks before the first real conversation. We removed that gap with AI-assisted intake, a same-day response standard, and contracting that happens in one place.`

Three items:

1. **AI-accelerated discovery** — Our AI assistant answers scoping questions the moment you ask them, then routes the details straight to a senior consultant — no waiting on a callback to get moving.
2. **Answers in hours, not weeks** — Every inquiry gets a human response in under one business day, with a scoped point of view — not a generic brochure.
3. **Contracting without friction** — Proposals, agreements, and e-signature run through our client portal, so you go from conversation to kickoff in days.

> **Decision needed.** Point 1 promises a live AI assistant. Squarespace cannot
> run one natively. Either connect a third-party chatbot (Chatbase, Tidio,
> Intercom — needs the Core plan for the embed) or reword this point. Do not
> publish the claim without the widget behind it.

## 10. FAQ

Theme: **Lightest 1**. Use an **Accordion block** — the stylesheet already
styles it (red icon, Sora titles, hairline dividers).

- Eyebrow: `QUICK ANSWERS`
- Heading 2: `Questions, answered fast.`
- Intro: `Anything not covered here gets a human reply in under 1 business day.`

1. **How quickly can we get started?** — Most engagements begin with a 30-minute discovery call within 48 hours of your first message. From there we can issue a scoped proposal and agreement through our client portal — many clients are at kickoff inside two weeks.
2. **What does a typical engagement look like?** — We scope to the outcome, not the hours. Engagements usually run as a short assessment sprint (2–4 weeks), a defined delivery phase, and an improvement cycle. Advisory retainers are available for ongoing decision support.
3. **How do contracts and agreements work?** — Proposals, statements of work, and agreements are issued through our secure client portal, with e-signature and milestone-based invoicing. You review everything in one place before anything is countersigned.
4. **Do you work with government and regulated organizations?** — Yes. A large share of our work is with public sector, healthcare, and regulated environments where governance, documentation, and audit-readiness are part of the delivery standard.
5. **How is AI used in your work?** — Two ways. Internally, AI accelerates research, analysis, and documentation so our consultants spend their time on judgment rather than assembly. Externally, our assistant answers your scoping questions instantly and hands off the specifics to a human on our team.
6. **What if we are not sure what we need yet?** — That is a normal starting point and a good use of a first conversation. Bring the problem, the constraint, or the deadline — we will tell you candidly whether we are the right fit and what we would do first.

## Contact page

New page, `/contact`. Hero section (**Darkest 1**) then a **Form block**.

Hero:
- Eyebrow: `LET'S WORK TOGETHER`
- Heading 1: `Ready to Move Forward?`
- Paragraph: `Tell us about your challenge, initiative, or opportunity. Quantum District is ready to help you define the path forward.`

Form fields — matching the Next.js build:

| Field | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Full name | Text | Yes |
| Work email | Email | Yes |
| Organization | Text | No |
| Phone | Text | No |
| What do you need help with? | Dropdown | No |
| Estimated budget | Dropdown | No |
| Timeline | Dropdown | No |
| Tell us about the challenge | Textarea | Yes |

**Dropdown — what do you need help with:** Strategic Consulting · Technology
Transformation · Program & Project Management · Data & Analytics · Process
Optimization · Implementation Support · Not sure yet — need guidance

**Dropdown — estimated budget:** Under $25,000 · $25,000–$75,000 ·
$75,000–$250,000 · $250,000+ · To be determined

**Dropdown — timeline:** Immediately · Within 30 days · This quarter ·
Exploring options

Submit button: `START A CONVERSATION`

**Form settings** (Form block → Storage / Advanced):
- Storage: email notification to the client's inbox, plus Google Sheet if wanted
- Post-submit message: `Thanks — we have your message. A senior consultant will reply personally in under one business day.`

> **What is lost versus the Next.js build:** the branded HTML notification, the
> red PRIORITY flag on urgent timelines, `Reply-To` set to the lead, and the
> branded confirmation email. Squarespace sends its own plain notification.
> To restore them, pipe submissions through Zapier or Make into Gmail using the
> templates in `src/lib/mailer.ts` of the Next.js repo.
