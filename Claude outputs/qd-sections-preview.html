Ask us directly
How quickly can we get started?

Most engagements begin with a 30-minute discovery call within 48 hours of your first message. From there we can issue a scoped proposal and agreement through our client portal — many clients are at kickoff inside two weeks.

What does a typical engagement look like?

We scope to the outcome, not the hours. Engagements usually run as a short assessment sprint (2–4 weeks), a defined delivery phase, and an improvement cycle. Advisory retainers are available for ongoing decision support.

How do contracts and agreements work?

Proposals, statements of work, and agreements are issued through our secure client portal, with e-signature and milestone-based invoicing. You review everything in one place before anything is countersigned.

Do you work with government and regulated organizations?

Yes. A large share of our work is with public sector, healthcare, and regulated environments where governance, documentation, and audit-readiness are part of the delivery standard.

How is AI used in your work?

Internally, AI accelerates research, analysis, and documentation so our consultants spend their time on judgment rather than assembly — that is how we keep turnaround short. For clients, we assess where AI creates real leverage in your operations and where it does not.

What if we are not sure what we need yet?

That is a normal starting point and a good use of a first conversation. Bring the problem, the constraint, or the deadline — we will tell you candidly whether we are the right fit and what we would do first.
